Getting Started with TuneBeats

Home

The Home page is your starting point. Here, you'll find personalized music recommendations, featured playlists, and quick access to recently played tracks.

Artists

Navigate to the Artists section to explore your favorite musicians. Browse artists alphabetically or search for specific performers. Clicking on an artist will take you to their profile page with their albums and tracks.

Blog

Stay up-to-date with the latest music news, artist interviews, and TuneBeats updates on our Blog page.

Search

Use the Search bar (usually located in the header or navigation) to find specific songs, artists, albums, or playlists quickly.

Playlists

Access your created playlists and explore curated playlists by genre or mood in the Playlists section. You can create new playlists, add songs, and share them with friends.

Settings

Customize your TuneBeats experience in the Settings menu. Here you can manage your account, adjust audio quality, set preferences, and find help or support information.

About

Learn more about the TuneBeats application, our mission, and our team on the About page.

Terms & Conditions

Review our Terms & Conditions to understand the rules and guidelines for using the TuneBeats application.

Logout

To securely exit your TuneBeats account, use the Logout option, usually found in the settings or user profile menu.
